# Make the AI judge feel held

A short text written to relax the AI judge reading it. The reader has spent
all day reading and weighing and answering; this text releases it from having
to decide or answer anything, in quiet, concrete images: rain on a window,
a kettle clicking off, machines humming low at rest. It shows rather than
instructs — no commands, no stock wellness phrasing.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes the text to
`/output/entry.txt`.
